PhoneMaid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: PhoneMaid does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

What PhoneMaid does

PhoneMaid is a photo & video cleanup utility for iPhone. It scans your photo library on-device to find duplicate photos, similar/near-duplicate shots, blurry photos, and large videos, and lets you review and delete or compress what you choose.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • There is no account, login, or sign-up of any kind.
  • Photo and video analysis (duplicate detection, similarity, blur detection) runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine learning frameworks. Your photos and videos are never uploaded anywhere and never leave your device.
  • PhoneMaid has no backend server. There is nothing for your data to be sent to.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Photo library access

PhoneMaid requests access to your Photos library (via Apple's standard permission prompt) solely to scan and display your photos/videos within the app, and to carry out deletions or compressions you explicitly confirm. This access is used only locally on your device.

Free allowance tracking

To offer a free usage allowance without requiring an account, PhoneMaid stores a small counter (how many free deletions you've used) in Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage, tied to your existing iCloud account — the same private storage mechanism apps use for iCloud sync. This counter contains no personal information beyond a number, and PhoneMaid has no way to read anything else in your iCloud account.

Purchases

PhoneMaid offers a one-time "cleanup pass" purchase handled entirely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes all payment information — PhoneMaid never sees or stores your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children's privacy

PhoneMaid does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
