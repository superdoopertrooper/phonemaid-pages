Common questions

Where did my deleted photos go?
Deleted items move to your device's Recently Deleted album for 30 days before space is actually freed, same as Apple's own Photos app. From Settings or a result screen you can open Recently Deleted directly to empty it sooner.

How does the free allowance work?
Every install gets a generous one-time free allowance of deletions — not a daily limit. Once it's used up, a single one-time purchase ($4.99) unlocks unlimited cleanup — no subscription, ever. Scanning and browsing results is always free.

I purchased the cleanup pass on another device / reinstalled — how do I get it back?
Open Settings → Restore purchase inside the app.

Does PhoneMaid upload my photos anywhere?
No. All scanning happens on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
